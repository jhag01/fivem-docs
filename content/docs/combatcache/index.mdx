combatCache tracks player combat hits and gives staff and on-duty emergency services a viewable log of who hit whom, with what, and how hard. The server is the source of truth for every entry — health, armor, distance, and speed are all read from the server's own tracked state, never trusted from a client's report, and a hit only commits once the server has independently corroborated it actually happened.

<ResourceLinks repo="combatCache" />

## Features

- Server-authoritative combat log — clients report hits, they don't hold or serve their own copy
- Corroboration — a hit only commits once the other participant's own client also reports it, or after a short grace period if it doesn't (logged either way, but marked unconfirmed if it wasn't)
- Plausibility checks reject a reported fatal hit the server's own health tracking can't verify
- Framework auto-detection — supports Qbox and ESX out of the box
- Staff and on-duty emergency-job (police/ambulance) access, checked both ace- and framework-group-based
- Optional ox_target integration so emergency jobs can check a player's injuries in-character
- Configurable instant-headshot-kill mechanic with a per-bone chance
- Fully configurable log columns and view modes (default/full/emergency)
- Debug logging and test-data commands for development

## Requirements

<Cards>
  <Card title="ox_lib" href="https://github.com/overextended/ox_lib" />

Also requires a supported framework: **Qbox** (`qbx_core`) or **ESX** (`es_extended`).

Optional: [ox_inventory](https://github.com/overextended/ox_inventory) for accurate weapon/ammo labels (a bundled fallback list is used without it), and [ox_target](https://github.com/overextended/ox_target) for the in-game "Check injuries" option — the whole integration can be disabled if you don't use it.
